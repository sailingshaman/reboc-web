How it works

Reboc is the contracting party on both sides of every assignment.

  1. Back to back contracts. Reboc contracts the specialist's company and the client separately, and is a party to both.
  2. Delivery. The agreed services are delivered under those contracts.
  3. Invoicing and payment. Reboc invoices the client, and pays the specialist once the client has paid.

Clients and specialists

The same structure, seen from either side of the agreement.

For clients

  • One contracting partyOne supplier, one invoice, one point of accountability.
  • Services, not peopleReboc contracts to deliver the agreed services, not to supply people to work under your direction.
  • DocumentationContract documentation is available for your own supplier review. We describe how we contract; we do not give legal assurances.

For specialists

  • What we expectYour own company, your own insurance and equipment, other clients alongside ours, and your own tax position.
  • Administration handledContracting, invoicing and the paperwork that comes with an assignment.
  • How payment worksWe pay for accepted work, after the client has paid us. This is set out in the contract.

About

Reboc has worked from The Hague since 2004. We are a subcontractor to the professional services organisations of enterprise software vendors, delivering through independent specialists who each work through their own company.

We contract the specialist and the client separately, deliver the agreed services, and handle the administration. We do not direct how the work is done.
