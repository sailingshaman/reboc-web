Privacy Notice

Version 2.0, 7 November 2026

1. Who we are

Reboc B.V. is a company registered in The Netherlands (KvK/Chamber of Commerce: 27263706), based at Goudenregenplein 61, 2565 GK Den Haag. We deliver IT services to our clients through independent specialists, each contracted through their own company, and we handle the related contracting, invoicing and administration.

For questions about this privacy notice or how we handle your data, you can reach us at info@reboc.nl.

2. What data we collect

We collect personal data in the following ways:

Through the contact form on our website:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The subject and content of your message

Through business interactions:

  • Name and contact details of independent specialists and client representatives
  • Professional information such as CVs, experience, and skills relevant to IT assignments
  • Company and billing information needed for contracts and invoicing
  • Records of the hours and work invoiced for an assignment
  • Intake documents of the specialist's company, such as Chamber of Commerce extracts, professional liability insurance, and evidence that the specialist works for other clients (client name and date)
  • Answers to periodic check-in questions about how an assignment is carried out
  • Signed documents and records of the work delivered under an assignment

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools on this website. We do not collect data automatically beyond what is technically necessary to serve the website (standard server logs processed by Cloudflare).

3. Why we collect it

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries submitted through our contact form
  • To enter into and manage our agreements with specialists and with clients
  • To handle invoicing and administration
  • To assess and document that each engagement is a contract for the delivery of services
  • To meet legal and tax obligations

4. Legal basis

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process your data based on:

  • Legitimate interest - to respond to your contact form submissions
  • Contract performance - to fulfil our obligations when you engage our services
  • Legitimate interest - to assess and document that each engagement is a contract for the delivery of services, which we need in order to account for the way we contract with independent companies
  • Legal obligation - to comply with Dutch tax and accounting requirements

5. Who we share data with

We may share your personal data with:

  • Our client, where this is necessary for the assignment and limited to what the assignment requires
  • Our accountant, for tax and financial reporting
  • Formspree Inc. (USA), which processes contact form submissions on our behalf
  • Cloudflare Inc. (USA), which proxies and serves this website, including the contact form, and processes standard server logs. The specialist portal does not run through Cloudflare
  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany), which hosts the specialist portal at portal.reboc.nl. Submissions are encrypted for our own system and deleted from the portal once we have collected them, and the portal itself keeps no email addresses
  • Proton AG (Switzerland), which sends portal and check-in email on our behalf

We do not sell your data to third parties.

6. International transfers

Some of our service providers (Formspree, Cloudflare) are based in the United States. These transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, including Standard Contractual Clauses as approved by the European Commission. Proton AG is based in Switzerland, which the European Commission has recognised as providing an adequate level of data protection. The specialist portal is hosted within the European Union.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Contact form submissions: deleted within 12 months of the last interaction, unless a business relationship is established
  • Contract and invoicing data: retained for 7 years after the end of the engagement, as required by Dutch tax law
  • Records of hours and invoiced work: retained for 7 years after the end of the engagement, as they form part of our tax records
  • Intake documents, check-in answers, signed documents and records of delivered work: retained for 7 years after the end of the engagement, as they form part of the administration we are required to keep under Dutch tax law
  • CVs and professional information where no engagement follows: deleted within 12 months

A specialist can request a copy of the records we hold about them at any time, using the contact details above.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (where legally permitted)
  • Object to or restrict the processing of your data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • File a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@reboc.nl.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal data. Our website is served over HTTPS and hosted on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Access to personal data within our organisation is limited to those who need it for their work.

10. Changes to this notice

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. Changes will be published on this page. We recommend checking this page occasionally.
